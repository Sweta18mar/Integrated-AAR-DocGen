# Project brief: Warehouse modernisation

Sponsor: Operations director. Budget approved: $2.0M capital over three years.

Goals: cut pick errors by 40%, support the new Leeds distribution site from Q3 2027, and
retire the 2009 in-house stock system, which is out of vendor support.

Constraints: customer data must stay in the EU/UK region. Go-live must avoid the November
to January peak season. IT can provide two integration engineers part-time.

Contact for questions: ops-programme@example.org.
