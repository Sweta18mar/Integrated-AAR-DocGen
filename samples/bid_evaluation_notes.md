# Bid evaluation notes: Warehouse management system (RFP-2026-041)

Evaluation panel met on 3 and 5 November 2026. Panel lead: Priya Nair (priya.nair@example.org, +1 415 555 0142).

## Bidders

Three vendors submitted compliant bids by the 31 October deadline.

**Northwind Systems** bid $1.82M over three years, including implementation and 24/7 support.
They proposed a 14-week rollout and named two reference customers in retail logistics.
Their solution already integrates with our ERP. Weakness: support is offshore only, and their
data-residency answer was vague.

**Contoso Ltd** bid $1.45M, the lowest price. Rollout estimate is 22 weeks. Contoso's demo
crashed twice during the barcode-scanning scenario. Their bid excluded data migration, which
the panel estimates would add about $180K.

**Fabrikam Inc** bid $2.10M. Strongest technical score: native RFID support and a proven
multi-site deployment at a company of our size. Rollout 16 weeks. Their contract terms include
a 9% annual price escalator, which the panel flagged as a risk.

## Scores (out of 100)

| Vendor | Technical | Cost | Delivery | Risk | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Northwind Systems | 78 | 82 | 85 | 70 | 79 |
| Contoso Ltd | 61 | 95 | 55 | 58 | 67 |
| Fabrikam Inc | 92 | 68 | 80 | 74 | 79 |

## Panel discussion

Northwind and Fabrikam tied on total score. The panel leaned towards Northwind on cost and
ERP integration, but wants written commitments on data residency before award.

Lessons for next time: the RFP did not require bidders to price data migration separately,
which made Contoso's bid look cheaper than it is. Demo scripts were shared only three days
in advance, which bidders said was too short.
